## Description (4000 characters max)

**Bolo** (बोलो - Hindi for "speak") transforms your voice into polished, ready-to-send text in any app on your Mac.

Stop typing. Start speaking.

### Why Bolo?

Typing is slow. Your thoughts move faster than your fingers ever could. Bolo bridges that gap—just hold a key, speak naturally, and watch your words appear exactly where you need them, cleaned up and formatted perfectly.

### How It Works

**Push-to-Talk:** Hold the fn key, speak your mind, release. Your words appear instantly—no filler words, proper punctuation, perfect grammar.

**Long-Talk Mode:** Press fn + Space for longer dictation sessions. Brain dump into emails, documents, or notes without touching the keyboard.

**Command Mode:** Select any text and speak a command like "make this more formal" or "summarize this." Bolo rewrites it instantly.

### Works Everywhere

Bolo works in every app on your Mac:
• Email (Mail, Gmail, Outlook)
• Messaging (Slack, Discord, WhatsApp, iMessage)
• Documents (Word, Google Docs, Notion, Obsidian)
• Code editors (VS Code, Cursor, Xcode)
• Social media (Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook)
• Any text field, anywhere

### Intelligent Processing

Bolo doesn't just transcribe—it thinks:

✓ **Removes filler words** — No more "um," "uh," "like," or "you know"
✓ **Adds punctuation** — Periods, commas, and question marks in the right places
✓ **Fixes grammar** — Subject-verb agreement, tense consistency, sentence structure
✓ **Course correction** — Say "no wait" or "actually" and Bolo keeps only what you meant
✓ **Context-aware** — Formal in emails, casual in messages, technical in code

### Personal Dictionary

Bolo learns your vocabulary:
• Names of colleagues, clients, and friends
• Industry jargon and technical terms
• Acronyms and abbreviations
• Anything you use regularly

### Privacy First

Your voice, your data. Bolo only processes audio when you're actively recording. We don't store your recordings or transcriptions on our servers.
